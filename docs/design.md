## Effective design

2026-10-20 confirmed reading preferences: prioritize compact comparisons with primary values, progress and observation time carrying their own meaning. Put traceability in accessible details; retain nearby explanations when a failure or limitation changes what the reader may conclude or do. Do not repeat a conclusion already clear from the value, time or group context. A missing machine association can use a neutral dash without implying a missing quota reading. On wide screens use available horizontal space within each complete row; narrow layouts must retain the same identity, values and actions. Disclosure controls for neighboring groups share an alignment, and selection controls retain accessible names without repeating their affordance in every row.

For comparable rankings, reuse Overview's horizontal-bar presentation: readable category, thick solid bar, numeric ticks and a value at each bar end, with relevant drill-through and secondary detail still available. This refers to the rendered chart, not thin progress tracks; the current reference has no grid lines. For session browsing, use the grouping and totals defined in the product spec. These preferences refine the Pulse layer below; they do not change accounting, account identity or the independent scope of account operations.

Agent-only trend series use shared identity colors: Claude Code is blue (`#2a78d6`) and Codex is orange (`#eb6834`) in Overview and Explore, regardless of data order, time range or agent filter. Joint agent/project/model groups retain separate series colors to distinguish combinations.

When a displayed quota reading comes from a machine reported as signed out, the row says “来源机器未登录”. With the observation disclosure closed, the age and its status note share a line when space permits and wrap naturally when it does not. Expanded observation details retain their own lines. The disclosure identifies the source machine, explains that the last reading remains displayed, and gives the login-and-refresh step for continuing updates from that machine. This is a source-machine state, not a claim that the account has expired or is unavailable everywhere.

The effective design is the Philo Pulse workspace, adapted directly from authenticated live observations of `/`, `/dashboard/` and `/pages/` on 2026-10-19. This is a project-local design, not a newly published template. [Decision 8e3a](adr/20261019-8e3a-pulse-reference-ui.md) supersedes Ark appearance and shell placement while preserving behavior. Pulse's independently authored report pages do not define the workspace palette. The prior template definitions and observations below remain historical evidence.

`web/styles.css` is the shared entry point, including dynamic details. All nine content pages place brand, console/guide switch, search and navigation in a persistent white 208px rail; the main area uses the full remaining width on `#f4f5f6`. This adapts Pulse's observed 180px rail to Monitor's labels. White cards use `#e2e4e7` borders and 6px corners; primary actions use `#0969da`, body ink `#1f2328` and supporting ink `#59636e`. Body text is system 14/21px, page headings in the workspace banner 600 14/20px, sections 600 16/24px, supporting prose 13/21px, compact labels 12px, and primary readings 32/40px with tabular numerals. Controls are 36px high, with 6px corners. Main padding is 0/28px/60px; the full-width white page banner is at least 72px high with 16px vertical padding and a bottom divider, followed by 28px of canvas spacing. Monitor retains its chart series and semantic status colors.

Overview groups its cost heading and three figures in one white card with the standard border, radius and padding. Desktop figures use three columns with vertical separators; narrow screens stack them with horizontal separators. The trend remains a separate white card. Quota comparison and account operations share a white section while keeping their independent task order. Explore, Sessions, Collection, Network and Guide use the same card spacing and typography. Desktop area-switch links are compact segmented controls rather than a separate top bar. At ≤900px, the shell becomes a sticky compact header and horizontally scrollable local navigation; main padding becomes 0/16px/40px. Session filters retain two columns, and existing labelled mobile tables keep every field. The light-only theme remains intentional; there is no theme selector or new background work.

The synthetic regression follows current quota provenance placement and filter → comparison → account-operation order; old assertions for identity-column provenance and a batch panel before filters were retired because the October 18 decisions already replaced those layouts.
